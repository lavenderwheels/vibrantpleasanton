Our priorities

01 /

A more walkable and bikeable Pleasanton

Promoting pedestrian and bike friendly development including safer crossings and sidewalks, traffic calming, and protected bike lanes.

  • Gather resident input on Pleasanton’s walkability and bikeability, focusing specifically on downtown and its connections, to better advocate for and implement temporary (to permanent) traffic calming and pedestrian measures.
  • Research the feasibility of creating a community-led temporary (to permanent) pedestrian-friendly infrastructure.
  • Support the city’s proposed downtown curb extensions.
  • Gather resident feedback on the city’s proposed protected bike lanes on Peters Ave.

    02 /

    A thriving downtown

    Advocate for policies that enable small businesses to succeed downtown. Create a beautiful, lively downtown that residents enjoy coming to for their everyday needs.

    • Reduce empty storefronts.
    • Survey residents and visitors about downtown.
    • Organize a pop-up event that supports local businesses and brings more people downtown.
    • Survey businesses about what policies, events, and actions help or hurt their businesses.
    • Work with PDA and city to study and draft a plan to create shared parking for downtown businesses.
    • Work with the city and nearby businesses to help secure a lease for a vacant storefront, prioritizing a market or family-friendly business.
    • Bring back the Antique Faire.
    • Increase the shade canopy downtown.

    03 /

    A connected community

    Build more public spaces, like plazas and parks, that allow for people of all ages to come together.

    • Attract businesses that will open community focused spaces like beer gardens, restaurants with outdoor seating and kids play spaces.
    • Host a pop-up event at Pleasanton's Nature House.
    • Host at least three events that foster community-building .
    • Work with the city to identify a location for a public plaza downtown.

  • list item one

    04 /

    More affordable housing

    Advocate for smaller, denser housing that makes Pleasanton more affordable.

    • Advocate for smaller, denser housing and mixed-use development, including multifamily housing downtown.
