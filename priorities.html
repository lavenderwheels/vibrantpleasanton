Our priorities

Practical changes that make Pleasanton better for the people who live here.

To kick things off, we’re focusing our 2027 work on the most recognizable part of Pleasanton, our downtown.

We’ve zeroed in four main priorities to help guide us. Check out our 2027 goals, how we plan to track progress, and how you can help.

01

A more walkable and bikeable Pleasanton

Safer crossings and sidewalks, slower traffic, better bike routes and parking, and stronger connections to surrounding neighborhoods and parks.

Our next steps

  • Gather resident feedback on proposed Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan to better advocate for and implement temporary traffic calming and pedestrian measures.
  • Work with the city on the Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan to improve Main Street's walkability, including advocating for curb extensions.
  • Work with Bike Pleasanton to create a bike connectivity map.

Priorities for 2027 · Advocacy and planned action

Ways to track progress

  • Track improvements to crossings, sidewalks, and bike connections.
  • Gather feedback on how comfortable people feel walking and biking downtown.
  • Note progress on a bike connectivity map and Main Street improvements.

Suggested indicators to guide our work, rather than reported results.

Bring your local knowledge

  • Tell us about a difficult crossing or a missing connection.
  • Share a route you use to reach downtown on foot or by bike.

02

A thriving downtown powered by small businesses

A mix of independent businesses, fewer empty storefronts, and more places to shop and run everyday errands.

Our next steps

  • Foster relationships with PDA and current downtown businesses.
  • Survey residents and visitors on how downtown is currently being used and what people would like to see change.
  • Organize a pop-up event that supports local businesses and brings more people downtown.
  • Take stock of the current state of downtown, including surveying what policies, events, and actions help or hurt businesses.
  • Work with the city and nearby businesses to help secure a lease for a vacant storefront, prioritizing a market or family-friendly business.

Priorities for 2027 · Advocacy and planned action

Ways to track progress

  • Track pop-up events and participation by local businesses.
  • Observe changes in empty storefronts and the mix of everyday businesses.
  • Ask residents which shops, services, and family-friendly activities they would use.

Suggested indicators to guide our work, rather than reported results.

Bring your local knowledge

  • Tell us which everyday businesses or activities you wish were downtown.
  • Connect us with an independent business interested in downtown or a pop-up.

03

A connected community

Create public places where people of all ages want to visit AND stay.

Our next steps

  • Host at least three events that foster community-building while gathering feedback on resident/visitor wants and needs, as well as event successes and failures.
  • Work with the city to identify a location for a public plaza downtown.

Priorities for 2027 · Advocacy and planned action

Ways to track progress

  • Track potential locations and conversations about a downtown plaza.
  • Look for more seating, shade, play, and gathering opportunities.
  • Ask neighbors how they use public spaces and what would help them stay longer.

Suggested indicators to guide our work, rather than reported results.

Bring your local knowledge

  • Suggest a downtown place that could become more welcoming.
  • Help with seating, shade, or activities at a pop-up gathering.

04

Affordable and dense housing

Advocate for denser and mixed-use housing in areas of Pleasanton where connectivity already exists. Promote the benefits of re-zoning and reuse rather than (sub)urban sprawl.

Our next steps

  • Work with the city to advocate for and identify sites in Pleasanton that are appropriate for rezoning and reuse.
  • Advocate for increased housing and mixed-use development downtown.

Priorities for 2027 · Advocacy and planned action

Ways to track progress

  • Track conversations about potential sites for rezoning and reuse.
  • Follow proposals for housing and mixed-use development downtown.

Suggested indicators to guide our work, rather than reported results.

Bring your local knowledge

  • Share ideas for suitable locations for housing and mixed-use development.
  • Join conversations about making Pleasanton affordable for young families.
